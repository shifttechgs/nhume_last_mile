# Nhume Business Case

## Executive Summary

Every day, thousands of vehicles travel between Zimbabwean cities with unused cargo space while thousands of parcels sit in courier depots waiting for the next scheduled dispatch.

Nhume is a logistics marketplace that matches parcels with transport already heading to the same destination. Instead of waiting for tomorrow's courier truck, a parcel can travel on a journey that is already happening today.

We are not trying to replace courier companies. We are making existing transport capacity more accessible, more efficient, and more profitable.

---

# The Problem

## The Customer Problem

Imagine you need to send an urgent parcel from Harare to Bulawayo.

You arrive at a courier office at 11:00 AM.

The response is:

> "The truck leaves tonight."

or

> "Delivery will be tomorrow."

Meanwhile:

* Multiple buses have already left.
* Several Toyota Quantums are travelling the same route.
* Long-distance taxis are departing every hour.
* Trucks are already heading in that direction.

Your parcel waits—not because transport doesn't exist, but because it isn't connected to you.

The current system is built around courier schedules instead of available transport.

---

## The Transporter Problem

Every day, transport operators travel with unused luggage and cargo space.

Examples include:

* Intercity buses
* Shuttle services
* Toyota Quantums
* Long-distance taxis
* Logistics companies
* Freight operators

That unused space represents lost revenue.

The transporter has already paid for:

* Fuel
* Driver wages
* Maintenance
* Road tolls

Whether the space is empty or full, the trip still happens.

Nhume enables transport operators to earn additional income from capacity they already have.

---

## The Business Problem

Thousands of SMEs rely on moving products between cities.

Examples include:

* Online stores
* Spare parts suppliers
* Pharmacies
* Hardware stores
* Fashion retailers
* Agricultural suppliers

Current courier options often require businesses to work around courier schedules rather than their customers' expectations.

This creates delays, slower order fulfillment, and reduced customer satisfaction.

---

# Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Traditional courier companies operate on fixed collection and dispatch schedules.

Their network is designed around depots and warehouses.

The typical process looks like this:

Customer → Courier Branch → Warehouse → Scheduled Vehicle → Destination Branch → Recipient

While this model works well for high parcel volumes, it can introduce unnecessary waiting when transport is already available.

Nhume takes a different approach by connecting parcels to journeys that are already taking place.

---

# Our Solution

Nhume is a digital marketplace for parcel transportation.

It connects:

* People who need to send parcels
* Verified transport partners already travelling to the destination

Instead of asking:

> "When is the next courier truck leaving?"

Customers ask:

> "Who is already going there today?"

The result is a faster, more flexible, and more efficient way to move parcels.

---

# How Nhume Works

### 1. Customer Creates a Parcel Request

The sender enters:

* Pickup location
* Destination
* Parcel size
* Weight
* Delivery urgency

---

### 2. Available Journeys Are Identified

Nhume finds verified transport partners travelling along the same route.

Examples:

* Bus operators
* Shuttle services
* Long-distance taxis
* Fleet operators

---

### 3. Parcel Is Assigned

The transport partner accepts the parcel.

Pickup and handover are coordinated through Nhume.

---

### 4. Delivery Is Confirmed

The recipient receives the parcel.

Proof of delivery is recorded.

The transporter is paid.

Nhume earns a commission.

---

# Why This Model Works

Unlike ride-hailing platforms that create new journeys, Nhume uses journeys that already exist.

Every successful booking increases the value of an existing trip instead of creating additional vehicle movements.

This improves efficiency for both senders and transport operators.

# Competitive Advantage

Nhume does not aim to compete by owning a fleet.

Instead, it builds a network of transport capacity that already exists.

As more transport partners join, the platform becomes more valuable to senders.

As more senders join, transport partners receive more earning opportunities.

This creates a marketplace network effect.
